Mumbai Indians

The Mumbai Indians are a franchise cricket team based in Mumbai, Maharatra, India.
They compete in the indian Premier league (IPL) and are one of the most successful teams in the league's histroy,
having won the IPL title multiple times. Owned by India's biggest conglomerate, Reliance Industries, theyplay their home matches at the WankhedeStadium. The team is known for its star-studded lineup. passionate fan base, and aggressive playing style.
