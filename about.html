ABOUT THE FAMILY

One Team.
One Family.

Mumbai Indians has built one of the Indian Premier League's strongest identities through consistency, competitive spirit and a deep connection with its supporters.

This fan website gives every part of that experience a dedicated home: matches for the schedule, squad for the players, tickets for matchday planning, store for the MI Collection and this page for the story behind the journey.

The goal is simple: make every visit feel like stepping into the Blue Family rather than sending fans from page to page without context.
